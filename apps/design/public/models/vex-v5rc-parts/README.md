# VEX V5RC part models

303 GLB models converted from VEX V5 STEP/STP part files with Blender 5.2.1 LTS and the installed Blender Org STEP Importer 1.2.1 extension.

The original category folders are preserved. `manifest.json` maps each generated GLB to its STEP source and records mesh/object counts and output size. Multi-body models remain separate meshes with their imported materials.

The STEP files came from [TlorschCode/VEX-V5-Parts-Solidworks-Download](https://github.com/TlorschCode/VEX-V5-Parts-Solidworks-Download), whose README says its part models are unchanged from VEX V5 CAD product downloads. Field models were excluded. This set follows the VEX V5 CAD product list; competition legality was not independently checked against a season game manual.

The STEP Importer used its configured tessellation and material preferences, with Y-up correction, unit scale 1, and preserved source placement. Blender exported binary glTF (GLB).
